How your score is calculated

Fully transparent: every dimension is built from weighted criteria, and every result shows its evidence.

1. Criteria → dimension score (0-100)

Each dimension is a checklist of weighted criteria (for example, "ATS compatibility" is worth 25 of the 100 points in Recruiter Appeal). Rule-based checks are deterministic, so the same CV always gets the same rule score.

2. Rules + AI review

Rule-based checks contribute about 60% of a dimension score and an AI review of clarity, relevance and impact adds about 40%. If the AI service is unavailable, you still get the rule-based score and a visible notice.

5. Gaps & recommendations

Any criterion below 60% of its points is a gap. Gaps are ranked by (dimension weight × points lost) and the top five become your prioritised recommendations.

Fairness & privacy

We do not score name, gender, age, photo or institution prestige. Your CV file is processed in memory and never stored; results expire after 30 days and can be deleted anytime.
